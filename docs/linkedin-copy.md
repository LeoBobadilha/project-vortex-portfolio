# LinkedIn presentation

## Main post (1,510 characters)

Project Vortex examines whether discretionary Price Action concepts can be formalized as systematic, testable research.

The research draws on my prior study of Al Brooks, Thomas Bulkowski and candlestick material, organized into traceable references and hypotheses.

AI agents supported knowledge organization, strategy implementation, data processing, backtesting, risk analysis and audit. Work moved through versioned artifacts and recorded test results. I set the research direction, clarified market context, reviewed evidence and decided which hypotheses warranted further work. Strategic acceptance and validation remained human responsibilities.

Engineering challenges included translating visual context into causal rules, preserving chronological data availability, reconciling execution costs and comparing portfolio risk across versions. Hypotheses that did not hold up under retesting were not advanced.

The selected public reference is the P6+SIZE+CE historical backtest: R$23,551 gross, R$20,395 net C30, 324 portfolio records and 526 contract units. P6 is reported separately. These are historical simulation results, not live-account performance.

The Power BI report presents aggregate performance, drawdown and research evidence.

Live portfolio: https://leobobadilha.github.io/project-vortex-portfolio/
BI project: https://github.com/LeoBobadilha/project-vortex-bi-showcase

Historical and simulated performance is not indicative of future results. Live trading involves substantial risk.
