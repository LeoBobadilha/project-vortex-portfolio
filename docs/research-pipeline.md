# Autonomous research workflow

Research progressed across Intelligence, strategy implementation, historical data/replay, execution-engine research, risk/performance analysis, auditing and Paper Live preparation. Components handed work forward through research artifacts, implementation requests, versioned code and recorded decisions. This supported concurrent experimentation and reduced repeated manual implementation.

Autonomy was bounded: ambiguous financial context, strategic acceptance and authorization toward live trading remained human decisions. Paper Live is simulated validation; no real-money history is claimed.
