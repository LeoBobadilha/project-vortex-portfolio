# Knowledge processing and retrieval

Local Price Action books and complementary references were treated as source material. The Intelligence workstream organized extracted concepts and research observations into maintained documents, versioned strategy books, catalogs, indexes and decision records. Research agents consulted those artifacts when investigating chart behavior or proposing hypotheses. This is structured document processing and retrieval; it is not foundation-model training, fine-tuning or a verified vector-search/RAG architecture.

Natural-language ideas were translated into measurable candidate rules only after human interpretation of context. The research lead corrected ambiguities, explained why a visual pattern was or was not a valid opportunity, and approved the final operational interpretation. Candidate implementations were replayed, tested, compared, audited and retested; weak or inconsistent hypotheses were revised or rejected before portfolio consideration.
