# Public data provenance

Three approved aggregate CSV files are distributed in `public/data/`. The portfolio browser code fetches `monthly_portfolio_results.csv` and `benchmark_summary.csv`; `annual_portfolio_results.csv` is the third aggregate CSV and is not requested by that code. The files support historical portfolio views for P6+SIZE+CE and the separate P6 baseline. PSC reconciles to 324 records and 526 contract units: R$23,551 gross, R$3,156 C30 costs applied once and R$20,395 net.

The replay adds a separate research-only scaling case: 107 WIN$ M5 OHLC bars for the source market session date 30 September 2022, with translated prices and session-relative time. The date was cross-checked between two distinct entry rows in the frozen event ledger and the matching source candles. The source clock timezone is unauthenticated, so the date is not presented as a certified B3 local timestamp. Exact event timestamps, identifiers, unshifted prices, the ledger and the broader archive remain excluded. The two simulated one-contract legs reconcile to R$750.20 modeled gross and R$738.20 after the study's R$12 S2 assumption. This is not a PSC record; S2 is not C30. Publishing the date with recognizable OHLC geometry materially increases external correlation risk. See the replay methodology for source limitations.

Other case-study images mask exact dates, price scales, family identifiers and individual results. All case studies are selected examples, not a distribution sample.
