# Public data provenance

Only the three approved aggregate CSV files are loaded client-side. They present annual, monthly and benchmark-level historical backtest summaries for P6+SIZE+CE and the P6 baseline. P6+SIZE+CE values reconcile to 324 portfolio records and 526 contract units, with gross R$23,551, C30 net R$20,395 and costs R$3,156 applied once. No row-level trade records are embedded.

The charts are selected examples, not a distribution sample. Public versions mask exact time and price scales, family identifiers and individual results.
